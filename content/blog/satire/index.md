Technical writing with the seriousness removed, or rather, with the seriousness pushed far enough that it collapses on its own. Satire is a diagnostic instrument here: exaggerating a technology's rhetoric until the shape of its actual claims becomes visible.

Two of these were generated with Grok 3 from a one-line prompt and then edited for comic timing, which is itself part of the point.
